## 2. Research evidence levels

Har researched point ko in categories mein identify karein:

- **Officially documented:** Product owner ki official documentation mein directly described.
- **Official product overview:** Official marketing/product page par described; detailed technical behavior separately verify karna hoga.
- **Research grouping:** Multiple documented capabilities ko samajhne ke liye researcher ne ek heading ya group mein organize kiya hai. Ye zaroori nahi ki product menu mein exact same naam ho.
- **Not verified:** Reliable source se confirm nahi hua; fact ki tarah publish na karein.

## 3. Verification checklist

Har feature/page entry ke liye:
1. Official source URL.
2. Page / document title.
3. Product and module.
4. Feature ka short, original Hinglish explanation.
5. Evidence level from section 2.
6. License / subscription dependency, if stated by source.
7. Region, tenant or configuration limitations, if stated.
8. Research checked date.
9. Any unresolved uncertainty.

Agar official docs se claim confirm na ho, use “not verified” mark karein ya omit karein.

## 5. Copyright-safe research rules

1. Facts and general product capabilities ko apne original words mein summarize karein.
2. Source ka title aur URL cite karein; source text ko bulk mein copy na karein.
3. Third-party website ke paragraphs, screenshots, diagrams, videos, icons, logos, product UI, source code ya proprietary graphics repository mein reproduce na karein.
4. Short quotation sirf tab use karein jab genuinely zaroori ho, quotation marks aur clear source attribution ke saath; otherwise paraphrase karein.
5. Product names ko sirf identification / research context mein use karein. Apne project ko official, certified, partnered ya endorsed product imply na karein.
6. Apna logo, brand, writing, diagrams aur original analysis independently create karein.
7. Open-source material use karne se pehle uski exact license aur attribution conditions check karein.
8. Copyright notice ownership ko sirf original research notes par apply karta hai; third-party trademarks, docs aur referenced materials par nahi.
9. “Publicly accessible” ka matlab “copyright-free” nahi hota.
10. Ye research hygiene guidance hai, legal opinion ya copyright clearance guarantee nahi.

## 6. Product availability caveat

Microsoft Defender aur CrowdStrike Falcon ke features plan, license, purchased modules, product edition, region, tenant configuration, permissions aur integration status ke hisaab se differ kar sakte hain. Official documentation aur product release notes ko research update karte waqt dobara verify karein.
